💡 Viva & Presentation Highlights

🏆 Meta-Llama-3-8B: Highest Fluency & N-gram Overlap

Scored 1st place in chrF++ (42.50), ROUGE-1 (52.77), ROUGE-2 (30.08), and ROUGE-L (49.46) with 18.80 BLEU. The 8B parameter capacity faithfully captures complex Hinglish phrases.

⚡ mT5-Small: Best Efficiency & Highest BLEU

Scored highest overall BLEU (19.94) and BERTScore (0.9061) while running in only 649ms per sentence. Pre-trained on mC4 internet data which includes extensive Romanized code-mixed Hindi.

🇮🇳 Sarvam-1: Indic Morphology & Fluency

Achieved 41.56 chrF++ and 47.16 METEOR. Pre-trained on 2 trillion Indian tokens with a 131K Brahmic tokenizer, preserving nuanced Hindi syntax.

📈 SOTA Outperformance

Both Meta-Llama-3-8B (18.80 BLEU) and Google mT5-Small (19.94 BLEU) beat both published baselines: RCMT (14.00 BLEU in LREC-COLING 2024) and PACMANtrans (18.66 BLEU in ICON 2023).
