💡 Viva & Presentation Highlights

⚡ mT5-Small: Best Efficiency & Accuracy

Scored highest overall BLEU (54.11) and BERTScore (0.9071) while running in only 645ms per sentence. Pre-trained on mC4 internet data which includes extensive Romanized code-mixed Hindi.

🇮🇳 Sarvam-1: Unmatched Indic Grammar

Dominated character-level chrF++ (66.85) and bigram ROUGE-2 (26.56). Pre-trained on 2 trillion Indian tokens with a 131K Brahmic tokenizer, preserving nuanced Hindi syntax in translation.

📈 3.8× Improvement Over 2024 SOTA

Both fine-tuned architectures comprehensively outperformed the latest 2024 SOTA baseline (RCMT @ 14.00 BLEU in LREC-COLING 2024) by +38.66 and +40.11 BLEU points, and PACMANtrans (@ 18.66 BLEU) by +34.00 points.
